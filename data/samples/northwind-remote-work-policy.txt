NORTHWIND LOGISTICS - REMOTE WORK POLICY

This is a fictional policy document written for the doc-rag demo. Northwind
Logistics does not exist and none of these rules apply to anyone. The facts are
invented so you can check whether an answer is grounded in the document.

Document version 3.2, effective 1 April 2024. Owner: People Operations.

SCOPE

This policy applies to all salaried employees of Northwind Logistics in the
Riverside, Oakmont and Tallow Bay offices. It does not apply to warehouse,
dispatch or driver roles, which are covered by the Depot Operations Handbook.

Contractors are covered only where their statement of work says so in writing.

ELIGIBILITY

Employees may work remotely after completing 60 days of continuous service.
New joiners in their first 60 days work from an office at least four days a
week so that onboarding and safety training can be completed in person.

Remote work must be approved by the employee's direct manager and recorded in
Workday. Approval lapses if the employee changes role or manager, and must be
requested again within 15 working days of the change.

CORE HOURS AND AVAILABILITY

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's registered time zone. Employees
must be reachable on Slack and by phone during core hours and must respond to
direct messages within two hours during that window.

Meetings may not be scheduled before 08:30 or after 17:30 without the consent of
every attendee. Any meeting longer than 50 minutes must include a 10-minute
break.

OFFICE ATTENDANCE

Fully remote employees are expected on site for four days per quarter, which the
company calls anchor days. Hybrid employees attend the office two days per week,
on days agreed with their manager and published in the team calendar.

Travel time to an anchor day counts as working time. Northwind reimburses
standard-class rail fare and up to 95 dollars per night for accommodation when
the journey exceeds 150 kilometres each way.

EQUIPMENT AND EXPENSES

Each remote employee receives a one-time home office allowance of 650 dollars,
claimable in the first 90 days of remote work. The allowance covers a desk,
chair, monitor and lighting, and does not cover rent, utilities or furniture
removal.

Northwind provides a laptop, a headset and an external monitor on request. A
monthly internet contribution of 40 dollars is paid to employees who are
approved as fully remote, and is not paid to hybrid employees.

Damaged equipment must be reported to IT Service Desk within 48 hours. Lost or
stolen equipment must be reported within 24 hours and to the local police where
theft is suspected.

DATA SECURITY

Company data may only be accessed on Northwind-managed devices. Personal devices
may be used for Slack and email only, and only with the company mobile device
management profile installed.

Full-disk encryption and automatic screen locking after 5 minutes of inactivity
are mandatory. Public Wi-Fi may be used only through the company VPN.

Printing customer data at home is prohibited. Paper records that already exist
at home must be returned to an office shredding bin within 30 days.

WORKING FROM ANOTHER COUNTRY

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar
year, subject to written approval from People Operations and the Tax team at
least 21 days in advance. Approval is refused where it would create a permanent
establishment risk or a local payroll obligation.

Sanctioned countries are never approved. The list of approved countries is
maintained by the Tax team and reviewed every six months.

HEALTH AND SAFETY

Remote employees must complete the home workstation self-assessment in Workday
once a year and within 30 days of moving home. Northwind may ask for photographs
of the workspace, which are deleted after assessment.

Work-related injuries at home are reported through the same incident process as
office injuries, within 24 hours.

NON-COMPLIANCE

A first breach of this policy is handled as a documented conversation with the
employee's manager. A second breach within 12 months may lead to withdrawal of
remote-work approval. Security breaches are escalated immediately to the
Information Security team and may lead to disciplinary action.

QUESTIONS

Questions about this policy go to People Operations at the internal address
people-ops, or to the policy owner named on page one. The policy is reviewed
every 12 months and was last reviewed on 10 March 2024.
